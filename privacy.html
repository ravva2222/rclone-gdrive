rclone-gdrive - Privacy policy

rclone-gdrive is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. There are no other users.

The app accesses Google Drive only to upload, list, share and delete the owner's own files, at the owner's request.

Google user data is not collected, sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising or for any other purpose.

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own devices. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
